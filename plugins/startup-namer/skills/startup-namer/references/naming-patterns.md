# Naming patterns

Mix these across each batch of 20.

| Pattern | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Coined word | A new word that feels natural | Spotify, Twilio, Klaviyo |
| Portmanteau | Two relevant words blended | Pinterest (pin + interest), Groupon (group + coupon) |
| Metaphor | An abstract concept that evokes the product | Asana (yoga pose, flow), Palantir (seeing stone) |
| Truncation | A shortened real word | Tumblr, Flickr, Scribd |
| Compound | Two simple words joined | Salesforce, Dropbox, Mailchimp |
| Domain hack | The TLD completes the word | del.icio.us, bit.ly, hover.sh |
| Abstract | Short, punchy, memorable sound | Zuul, Klar, Vex, Novu |
| Mythological or literary | Mythology, science, or literature | Palantir, Janus, Hermes |
| Action word | A verb implying what the product does | Gather, Rally, Loom |
| Respelled | A common word spelled creatively | Lyft, Fiverr, Dialpad |

## Quality bar

- **Pronounceable**: someone can say it after reading it once.
- **Spellable**: someone who hears it can type it correctly.
- **Memorable**: two or three syllables, eight characters or fewer preferred.
- **Inoffensive**: no unfortunate meaning in major languages.
- **Distinct**: not easily confused with a major brand.
- **Relevant**: fits the vertical and the product.

## Domain hacks

Look for words whose ending is a real, registrable TLD, so the domain reads as the
whole word.

- Common endings: .io, .ai, .co, .do, .so, .to, .is, .it, .me, .us, .sh, .ly, .in, .at,
  .be, .de.
- Good: `launch.ai`, `snap.chat` (the .chat TLD exists), `gath.er` only if .er were
  registrable.
- Bad: splits that break the word, such as `real.iz` for "realize", or hacks on TLDs
  that do not exist or that restrict registration to residents.
- Check the exact hack domain with `check-domain.sh hack.tld`.
