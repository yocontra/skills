# The six upward sections

Draft each section from the user's own gathered activity and the
carry-over doc, then list the questions only the user can answer in
one closing batch. Never ask what a source answers. A section with
nothing to say stays short or empty; do not pad it.

Section order in the packet: loose ends fold into the others, then
Decisions, Asks, Team, Personal / Growth, FYI.

## 1. Loose ends from the last upward 1:1

**Sources:** unchecked items and open asks in the carry-over doc;
issues or PRs named there and their current state.

**Draft:** fold each open item into Decisions, Asks, or FYI with its
current status ("Resolved", "Punted", "Got harder"). An item punted
twice gets "Is the punt the decision? Say it out loud."

**Closing question, if still open:** "Anything you committed to your
manager last time that is not in your notes?"

## 2. Decisions needed

**Sources:** your open PRs or design docs waiting on sign-off; issues
labeled `needs-decision` or similar; threads where you are blocked on
someone above you.

**Draft:** one bullet per decision, tagged Input, Air cover, or
Sign-off, with your preferred direction if the record shows one. A
decision with no recorded preference gets `TODO: your call if it were
yours alone?`

**Closing question, if still open:** "Any decision you need from your
manager that does not show up in PRs, docs, or issues?"

## 3. Asks

**Sources:** cross-team blocks, escalations, headcount or priority
conflicts visible in issues and milestones.

**Draft:** one bullet per ask: of whom (your manager, their boss,
cross-team), and why now. An ask with no visible "why now" says so.

**Closing question, if still open:** "What do you need from your
manager this week that the data does not show (headcount, budget,
air cover)?"

## 4. Team

**Sources:** big shipping, incidents, stuck work, and team changes in
the window.

**Draft:** wins worth flagging and risks your manager should hear
from you first, each tagged Want input, Awareness, or Letting them
know. Apply the house-style test: would you say this bullet to their
face? Often the right answer is "nothing this week."

## 5. Personal / Growth

**Sources:** usually none. Leave the section as `TODO: anything you
want feedback on, or feedback to give?` rather than inventing content.
For `upward:2` and above, drop it unless the request raises it.

## 6. FYI

**Sources:** incidents, external pressure, or risks visible in the
window.

**Draft:** each item with its source and urgency (now, by next week,
on record).

## The closing batch

After the packet, one numbered list of the open questions above,
most meeting-changing first, at most five. Skip a catch-all "anything
else?". When the user answers, update the affected sections and
reprint them.
